The moon picked our dates.

The full moon lands on Christmas Eve, and it is the biggest supermoon of 2026. Here is how much of it we will see on each night of the trip.

Moonrise over the sea

A full moon rises in the east as the sun sets. Kalapathar on Havelock and Sitapur on Neil both face east, so on 23 and 24 December we can sit on the sand and watch it come up out of the water.

The lowest tides of the month

Full moons pull the sea far back. That is the best time to walk out to Neil's Natural Bridge and its rock pools. We'll check the tide table the evening before.

What we are skipping

Bioluminescence kayaking needs dark nights. With this much moonlight the glow is hard to see, so we save about ₹3,500 each and spend it on scuba instead.

Where we could stay

Family-friendly picks on each island, from simple to splurge. Prices are indicative for a double room per night and climb in the last week of December, so check live rates before booking. Photos show the area; tap "Room photos" for the hotel itself.

Christmas Eve catch: some resorts add a compulsory gala dinner on 24 December. One Havelock hotel lists ₹3,499 per adult. Ask before paying, and ask whether the gala menu has a proper veg spread.

Veg food, island by island

Pure veg kitchens first, then cafes that serve fish too but do excellent veg plates. On menus, look out for fish sauce and prawn stock in "Thai" dishes and ask.
